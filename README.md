## Hi there 👋

**Senior Technical Product Manager | AI, Data & Industrial SaaS**

I'm a product leader who spends most of my time thinking about how AI
systems make decisions that affect people's safety — and how to build
tools that keep them honest. 15+ years across product, data science, and
engineering got me here, by way of a Master of Information and Data
Science (MIDS) from UC Berkeley and an earlier life in industrial
engineering (water, utilities, manufacturing — the unglamorous stuff that
keeps the lights on).

### What I'm working on

Outside of my day job, I care a lot about the built environment — the
buildings we live and work in, and whether the systems meant to keep them
safe are actually working. That's the thread running through
[`housing-agent-eval`](https://github.com/autumninthecloud/housing-agent-eval),
where I'm testing whether multi-agent AI systems genuinely outperform
simpler ones on housing-safety analysis (spoiler: it's not as obvious as
the hype suggests), and [BobBuilderGPT](https://github.com/mike-khor/BobBuilderGPT)
(CodeQuery), an AI assistant for navigating building codes that I built
with a great team at a UC Berkeley hackathon.

If you're working on anything at the intersection of AI, safety, and the
places people live — I'd love to hear about it.
